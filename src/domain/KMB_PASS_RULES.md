# KMB Monthly Pass comparison

Verified on 2026-11-14 against KMB's terms dated 2025-11-14:
https://m.kmb.hk/files/20251114%20KMB%20MP%20(chi)%20P960P968.pdf

The normal adult comparison uses a valid pass already loaded on the same
Octopus, with sufficient remaining rides. The HK$834 purchase price is separate
from the per-journey payable fare. No government subsidy, temporary student
promotion, airport-staff rate or unverified interchange discount is included.

- KMB-operated regular, overnight, joint and racecourse services are included,
  excluding K12, K14, K17 and K18.
- LWB-operated E, N, S, R and X services are included.
- KMB HK1 and LWB A/NA services charge 27% of the adult fare. The displayed
  calculation rounds to the nearest HK$0.10 and is an estimate; the operator's
  final fare prevails.
- P960/P968 charge HK$5, capped at the lower normal fare for this comparison.
- Included rides assume the 10-ride daily quota remains; B1 has a separate
  two-ride quota. KMB defines the pass service day from 04:45 Hong Kong time.
- Citybus-operated joint departures and other operators stay payable. A joint
  departure without a verified operating company shows a min–max pass fare,
  rather than assuming a KMB/LWB vehicle. The first reachable live prediction
  can identify the company; future timetables alone cannot.

The original adult fare and ranking remain unchanged. Unknown adult fares stay
unavailable in both comparisons, and published full-fare upper bounds retain
their qualification. The comparison does not read a passenger's Octopus balance,
pass expiry, or earlier ride usage. Future policy changes require re-verification.
