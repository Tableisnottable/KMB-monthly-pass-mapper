# Travel passes and cards

Catalogue expanded on 20 November 2026 from public operator/government pages. No account, API key or paid data feed was used. This is a directory of known products, not a complete inventory of every Asian ticket or a guarantee of current availability.

KMB is the only product with automatic fare rules. Other entries record ownership only. Stored-value cards are labelled explicitly; they are not unlimited-travel passes. Eligibility, validity and current sales terms must be checked on the linked official page.
